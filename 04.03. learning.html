Security issues versus performance issues.

A network that is slow and a network that is under attack can look identical on the first screen you open. This Study Hub drills the method for telling the two apart with evidence, and then closes Topic 1 by pulling subjects 1.1 to 1.5 into one picture you can use in the labs, in the midterm, and on the job.

Quick Pro-Tip

A performance issue is explained by demand, faults and change. A security issue is explained by an actor. When nothing ordinary explains a symptom and traces of an actor are present, the classification is security — even when the service is still working perfectly.

The four-question triage

  1. What exactly is affected, and since when?
  2. Does demand or a change explain it?
  3. Is there evidence of an actor? (the decisive question)
  4. What does the baseline say?
