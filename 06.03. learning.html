Quick tip

Most mistakes in this class come from one of three confusions. Before answering, ask yourself which one the question is testing:

  • Constraint or requirement? Respect versus achieve.
  • Organizational or operational? Money, people, rules and laws — versus links, disks, machines and clocks.
  • Observable or data source? The sign with a number — versus the place where it is recorded.

And keep the storage formula at hand: bytes per event × events per second × 86,400 × days ÷ 1,000,000,000.
