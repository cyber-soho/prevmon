What to remember

Two inputs — resources and security requirements — produce one ordered, written list. Criticality is the highest of the three impacts (how bad); exposure is the sum of five observed facts (how likely); the priority score is criticality × exposure; the score gives the tier, the tier and the tie-breakers give the order, and the order is fitted into the resource envelope — reduce before you defer, and document every override in the monitoring plan.
