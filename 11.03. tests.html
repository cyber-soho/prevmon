How to study with this hub

  1. Read the class handout once, lesson by lesson.
  2. Go through the flash cards of one lesson. Say your answer aloud before you flip the card, then mark it I knew it or Review again.
  3. Take the quiz. Use the hint only after a first attempt at the answer; read the explanation of every option after checking.
  4. On the results page, open the questions you missed, then retry only those.
  5. Come back to the cards marked Review again the day before Quiz 3.
